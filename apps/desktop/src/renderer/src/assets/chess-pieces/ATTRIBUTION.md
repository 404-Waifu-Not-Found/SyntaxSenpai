Chess piece silhouettes

The six SVG silhouettes in this folder are the Font Awesome Free 6.7.2
`chess-bishop`, `chess-king`, `chess-knight`, `chess-pawn`, `chess-queen`,
and `chess-rook` icons by Fonticons, Inc.

Source: https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/tree/6.x/svgs/solid
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The icons are used as recolorable chess-piece silhouettes. They are not
affiliated with or endorsed by Chess.com.
